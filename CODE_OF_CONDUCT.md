# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

The full text is available at the link above. The short version:

- Be respectful and inclusive in all project spaces (issues, discussions, Discord, any other community venue).
- Assume good faith and respond constructively.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and trolling are not tolerated.
- Project maintainers are responsible for enforcement and may take action including warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans.

## Reporting

Violations should be reported to **conduct@kottos.ai**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated as confidential.

When reporting, please include:

- A description of what happened.
- Where it happened (issue link, discussion thread, etc.).
- Whether the behavior is ongoing.
- Any context that would help us understand the situation.

We commit to acknowledging reports within 2 business days and responding with our planned action within 7 business days.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, ranging from a private conversation to a permanent ban from project spaces. Decisions are at the maintainers' discretion.

For the full enforcement guidelines, see the [Contributor Covenant v2.1 Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
